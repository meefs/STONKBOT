How it works

Five steps, start to live.

  1. 01

    Register

    Reply register to @stonkfunbot. No form, no signup, no email.

  2. 02

    Get your agent wallet

    The bot generates a Solana keypair that belongs to your handle alone and replies with the address. The secret is encrypted before it is stored.

  3. 03

    Fund it

    Send SOL to that address. It covers StonkFun's launch cost, the STONKBOT fee and network fees. See the numbers →

  4. 04

    Launch

    Tweet launch <name> paired with <QUOTE>. The bot prices the launch, checks the transaction, signs with your wallet and submits.

  5. 05

    Claim your creator fees

    Your wallet is the creator address on chain. StonkFun splits the pool's trading fees 50/50 with creators; you claim your half from the token page.

Security

What actually protects your SOL.

Every item below is a mechanism in the code, not a slogan. Read them as a description of the design, and judge it accordingly.

Encrypted keys at rest

Agent wallet secrets are encrypted with Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC) under a master key held only in the server environment. The database stores ciphertext.

Transactions are checked before signing

The bot decodes StonkFun's payment transaction and refuses to sign unless your wallet is the fee payer, it is the only required signature, and the SOL leaving your wallet is within a hard cap.

One launch per command

Each launch is keyed to the tweet that asked for it. A repeated or replayed mention returns the original result instead of paying a second time.

Wallets are isolated per handle

A keypair is only ever loaded for the handle that owns it. There is no code path that signs for one user on another user's request.

Rate limits and a circuit breaker

Per-user and global rate limits, a daily launch budget, and a breaker that trips after repeated failures so a broken dependency can't be hammered.

Dry-run by default

The bot ships with live spending disabled. Turning it on is a deliberate, explicit configuration change.

Be clear-eyed about the trade-off. Your agent wallet's key is generated and held by STONKBOT's server so the bot can sign for you from a tweet. That is custody, and it means you are trusting the operator and the server's security. Fund it with what a launch needs — it is not a place to store SOL. Tokens can and do go to zero.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is STONKBOT?

An X bot that launches tokens on StonkFun for you. You tweet a command, it does the on-chain work, and your wallet ends up as the token's creator.

What is StonkFun?

A Solana launchpad where every token is paired against another token — commonly a tokenized stock like GMEX (GME) or NVDAX (NVIDIA) — instead of against SOL. Each launch opens a one-sided Raydium market. STONKBOT is an independent client of their public API.

What is an agent wallet?

A Solana keypair the bot creates for your X handle, used to pay for and sign your launches. It makes your handle the creator on chain. Its secret key is encrypted at rest and held on STONKBOT's server — see the trade-off noted in Security.

How much SOL do I need?

Whatever the bot quotes for your specific launch, plus the 0.1 SOL service fee and a little for network fees. Around 0.35 SOL is a comfortable starting balance, but treat that as a suggestion — the quote is the real number, and the bot will tell you if you're short before spending anything.

Why won't you just state a minimum?

Because we can't state one honestly. StonkFun prices each launch at request time inside the payment transaction rather than publishing a fixed fee. Printing a number here would mean guessing, and a guess about someone's money is worse than a clear "we quote it live".

What is the STONKBOT service fee?

0.1 SOL per successful launch, transferred from your agent wallet after your token is live. It is separate from anything StonkFun charges. If your launch fails, you are not charged it.

What share of creator fees do I get?

StonkFun's standard launch mode — the one STONKBOT uses — splits the pool's 1% trading fees 50/50 between the creator and the platform. Your wallet is the creator, so that half accrues to you and you claim it from the token page. It accrues only when people actually trade the token.

Where does the token launch, and what do I get?

On StonkFun, into a one-sided Raydium pool quoted against the pair you chose. You get a fixed-supply token with a live market and the creator fee position. You can optionally take a dev buy of up to 2.5% of supply as the pool's first trade.

Can I check my wallet balance?

Reply balance or whoami to the bot and it returns your address and current SOL balance.

What happens if a launch fails?

It depends on how it failed, and the bot distinguishes the cases rather than guessing. If StonkFun confirms nothing was charged, you can simply try again. If a payment landed but the launch needs manual recovery, the bot tells you not to retry — retrying there would mint a second token. Either way, no service fee is charged for a launch that didn't succeed.

How do referrals work?

Someone adds ref yourhandle to their launch command. When their launch succeeds, 30% of our 0.1 SOL fee — 0.03 SOL — goes to your agent wallet. One level only, no self-referrals. This is STONKBOT's own rebate, not a StonkFun program.

Is this financial advice?

No. Launching a token is not an investment and nothing here suggests you'll make money. Creator fees only exist if people trade. Most tokens go to zero. Only spend what you're willing to lose.
