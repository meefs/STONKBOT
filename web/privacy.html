Privacy & data protection

Your data, and what guards it.

STONKBOT holds a wallet on your behalf, so this page is written to be specific rather than reassuring. It describes what is stored, how it is protected, who else sees it, and what we cannot protect you from.

What we store

There is no account, no email address, no password, and no personal profile. Using the bot means tweeting at it, so the only identifier involved is the public X handle that sent the tweet.

We never ask for and never store a seed phrase, a private key of yours, an exchange credential, or a password. If anything claiming to be STONKBOT asks you for one, it is not us.

How it is protected

These are mechanisms in the running code, not aspirations.

Encrypted private keys

Every agent wallet key is encrypted with Fernet — AES-128-CBC with an HMAC-SHA256 authentication tag, so tampering is detected, not just hidden. The database stores ciphertext only.

Slow key derivation

The encryption key is derived from the master secret with scrypt and a random per-install salt. scrypt is memory-hard and deliberately slow, so a stolen database is expensive to attack offline rather than trivial.

The master key never touches disk

It lives only in the server's environment. It is not in the code, not in the repository, and not in any database. Without it the stored wallets are meaningless ciphertext.

Owner-only files

The data directory is created 0700 and every database file 0600 — readable only by the account running the bot, not by anything else sharing the host.

Wallets are isolated

A key is only ever decrypted for the handle that owns it. No code path signs for one user in response to another user's command.

Transactions checked before signing

Before your wallet signs anything, the transaction is decoded and rejected unless you are the fee payer, yours is the only signature required, and the SOL leaving your wallet is within a hard cap.

Nothing is charged twice

Each launch is tied to the tweet that requested it, so a repeated or replayed command returns the original result instead of paying again.

Secrets stay out of logs

Diagnostics print a redacted view of the configuration. Keys are not written to logs, error messages, or replies — a property checked by the test suite, not just by convention.

The website

  • No cookies. This site sets none, for any purpose.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no advertising pixels. Nothing profiles you here.
  • No wallet connection. The site never asks you to connect a wallet or sign anything. Everything happens on X.
  • No third-party requests from your browser. Fonts, styles, scripts and images are all served from this domain. A Content-Security-Policy blocks anything else at the browser level.
  • Live market data is proxied. The token board reads StonkFun's public API through our own server, so your browser never contacts StonkFun and your IP address is never exposed to them just for loading this page.
  • Token names are rendered as text, never as markup — they are chosen by whoever launched the token, so they are treated as untrusted input.

Who else sees it

We do not sell, rent, or share your data for marketing. It is not shared with anyone beyond what the product mechanically requires:

Keeping and deleting

Wallet records are kept for as long as the wallet may hold funds, because deleting the encrypted key would permanently destroy access to any SOL or tokens at that address. Fee records are kept as an accounting trail. Handled tweet IDs are pruned to a recent window.

To have your data removed, message @stonkfunbot on X. Move your funds out of the agent wallet first — once the key is deleted, anything left at that address is unrecoverable by anyone, including us.

What we cannot protect

Your agent wallet's key is held on our server. That is what lets the bot sign from a tweet, and there is no way around it in this design. Encryption protects that key at rest; it does not remove the fact that you are trusting the operator and the host. Fund the wallet with what a launch needs — it is not a place to store SOL.

  • Anyone who controls your X account can command your agent wallet. Secure your X account.
  • Blockchain transactions are public, permanent, and irreversible.
  • Token prices can go to zero, and most do. Nothing here is investment advice or a promise of income.
  • No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise.
